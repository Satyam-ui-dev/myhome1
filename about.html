About Home Control

Home automation dashboard

This website lets you control two lights, a fan, and a 5-pin socket connected to an ESP32.

The Analysis page shows each device's status, recorded ON time, daily usage, and recent activity.

Power ratings used for energy estimates

DeviceRated power
Light 115 W
Light 215 W
Fan60 W
5-pin socket100 W

Energy is estimated from recorded ON time: kWh = power in watts × hours ÷ 1,000. Actual consumption may differ from these rated-power estimates.
